Digital clutter produces low-grade cognitive friction. Every badge, unorganized folder, and subscription creates an invisible tether pulling at attention.

## A Three-Stage Reset

1. **The Desktop Sweep**: Move everything on your desktop into a dated folder titled `Archive-YYYYMMDD`. If you haven't opened a file within fourteen days, delete it.
2. **Notification Zero**: Turn off all banners, lock screen alerts, and red badge counters for every app except direct phone calls from family.
3. **Single Window Focus**: Hide menu bars and dock icons. Let only the active document breathe on screen.
