Modern electronics do not age; they deteriorate. The glued battery swells; the unrepairable OLED panel fractures; the cloud server certifying the license goes dark.

A leather notebook or a cast-aluminum mechanical watch ages gracefully. Every scuff, sun-fade, and hairline mark becomes a record of where it has traveled with you.

## Patina Over Obsolescence

Software can have patina too: configuration files refined over fifteen years, plain text files readable with standard UNIX utilities from forty years ago, and personal archives stored locally without algorithmic intermediaries.

When choosing our instruments, we should ask: *Will this tool still be faithful to me when the company that made it ceases to exist?*
