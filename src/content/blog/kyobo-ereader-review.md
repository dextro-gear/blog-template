Modern tablets are marvels of computational power, but tragic failures as reading sanctuaries. Even with "Do Not Disturb" engaged, the capacitive glass pulses with the latent potential of twelve thousand other applications.

The Kyobo eReader represents an alternate timeline: an e-ink device engineered with strict hardware limitations.

## Tactile Qualities

The physical casing is composed of a matte basalt polycarbonate with zero gloss. There is no camera bump, no speaker grille, and no notification LED. When the device sleeps, it displays an unlit woodcut print.

> "A tool should only do one thing with unflinching excellence."

Battery life stretches into three weeks of unhurried reading. The page turns have a subtle mechanical cadence, not unlike leafing through thin onion skin paper in an old archive.
