A mechanical keyboard allows the fingers to outrun the mind. We generate thousand-word drafts in minutes, mistaking throughput for clarity.

A fountain pen imposes friction.

Because ink cannot be backspaced, you are forced to construct the sentence in your mind before the nib touches the fibers of the page. This momentary latency—the three seconds spent framing an idea before committing pigment to cellulose—is where quality resides.

## The Chemistry of Slowness

Using iron gall ink or water-resistant pigment inks grounds the writer in the physical realities of surface tension, evaporation, and drying times. It reminds us that thoughts are artifacts of the physical world.
