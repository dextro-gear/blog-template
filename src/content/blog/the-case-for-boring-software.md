Exciting software demands that you reorganize your workflow around its latest paradigm. Boring software quietly stores plain text in folders on your hard drive.

Boring software doesn't rebrand every eighteen months. It doesn't deprecate keyboard shortcuts. It works when the Wi-Fi is down on a train passing through alpine valleys.

When the tool is unremarkable, all the creative energy flows into the artifact being made.
