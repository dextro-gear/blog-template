Books have back covers. Newspapers have a final page. Walking trails have trailheads and overlooks.

The infinite scroll is an architectural crime against human psychology. By removing the boundary where content naturally concludes, it removes the trigger for reflection.

## Reclaiming the Margin

Interfaces that know when to stop respect our mortality. When an application says, "You are all caught up for today," it grants the user permission to close the tab and return to the physical world.
