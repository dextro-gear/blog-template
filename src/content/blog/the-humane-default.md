Defaults are destiny. Ninety-five percent of users never alter the initial settings of an operating system or application.

When the default configuration of a messaging tool is set to sound an audio chime on every incoming ping, the design team has decided the cognitive baseline of millions of people.

## Inversion of Responsibility

A humane tool assumes silence by default. It asks permission to interrupt, not forgiveness for having shattered an afternoon of concentrated thought.

- Audio chimes disabled out of the box.
- Batch delivery of non-urgent updates.
- End-of-day digests instead of real-time firehoses.
